# Healthcare software services

Apptunix's healthcare page advertises software services for clinics, hospitals and health startups. Categories include AI-enabled healthcare applications, wearable integrations, electronic health and medical records (EHR/EMR), IoT-connected healthcare applications, on-demand doctor applications and pharmacy management systems.

The page describes appointment booking and video consultations for doctor applications. For EHR/EMR work it describes interoperability using HL7/FHIR. It also advertises cloud-based, AR/VR and blockchain healthcare solutions. These are public service descriptions and do not establish that a particular customer deployment has been independently certified or clinically validated.
