# Apptunix industries and public project FAQs

The official FAQ names healthcare, fintech, e-commerce, real estate, gaming, logistics, travel and hospitality, food delivery, education and on-demand services among Apptunix's industries.

Apptunix says some project information is protected by nondisclosure agreements; selected public case studies are available on its website. A public portfolio is not a complete list of all customers or confidential engagements.

The FAQ says Apptunix develops for both iOS and Android. It names Swift and Objective-C for native iOS, Kotlin and Java for native Android, and Flutter and React Native for cross-platform development.

For delivery methods, the FAQ describes predominantly agile development with iterations and client feedback, while noting that traditional approaches can also be accommodated.
