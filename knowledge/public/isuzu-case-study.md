# ISUZU sales reporting platform

Apptunix's public ISUZU case study describes a centralized web platform for sales reporting and forecasting across African dealerships. The listed services are web platform development, data integration, UI/UX design and deployment, in the commercial vehicle and automotive sector.

The case study describes separate dealer and administrator panels, sales submissions, review and approval workflows, notifications, historical data migration and Power BI dashboards. The narrative explicitly mentions MongoDB as a data source for analytics and AWS hosting.

## Launch status caveat

As checked on October 18, 2026, the case study labels launch as pending and says the platform is awaiting public launch. Do not infer a completed public launch from homepage marketing. The generic technology carousel lists many tools, so it is not treated as proof of the project's exact full stack. Outcomes are company-reported and are not independently audited here.
