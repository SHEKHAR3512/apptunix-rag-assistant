# Apptunix company overview

Apptunix describes its business as AI product engineering and software development. It builds digital systems for businesses. Its homepage presents software engineering, mobile applications, AI development, enterprise solutions and blockchain work.

## Mobile and software technologies

The homepage lists iOS, Android, Flutter, React Native and progressive web apps as mobile expertise. Its technology section includes Python, Node.js, Java, Go and FastAPI for backend work, and ReactJS, Angular, Next.js, Vue.js and TypeScript for frontend work. These are company-listed capabilities, not a promise that every project uses every technology.

## Evidence boundaries

The homepage contains company-reported marketing metrics. This summary intentionally omits exact staff, delivery and revenue statistics because they change and are not independently audited. The knowledge assistant is an independent demo and does not represent Apptunix support.
